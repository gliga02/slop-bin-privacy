FAQ

Common questions

Is this official LinkedIn?

No. Slop Bin is independent and not affiliated with LinkedIn. We just visually dismiss posts you click in your own browser.

Does it read or send my feed posts to a server?

No. The extension only modifies what you see in your own browser. The only outbound network call is to Gumroad over HTTPS when you activate a license key.

How do I redeem my license?

After purchase, Gumroad emails you a license key. Open the Slop Bin popup, paste the key into the License field, and click Activate. Unlimited unlocks immediately.

Can I use the same key on more than one computer?

Yes. Activate the same key in the popup on each device you use. Settings are local per browser, so it’s a one-step activation per machine.

Refunds?

Refunds are handled by Gumroad. If something’s wrong with activation, email itdjordjegligorijevic@gmail.com and we’ll sort it out.

Reddit? X (Twitter)?

Not yet. v1 covers LinkedIn only. The architecture is ready for more sites; we ship one cleanly at a time so the truck doesn’t miss a stop.

Is bin mode just for laughs, or does it actually help?

Both. Visually removing slop reduces the friction of scrolling without committing to a permanent “mute” or report. It’s a tiny ritual that breaks the doomscroll loop without lecturing you.
