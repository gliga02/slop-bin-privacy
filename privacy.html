Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 May 2026

This page explains what Slop Bin stores, what it sends over the network, and how to contact us. It is written in plain language; this is not legal advice. If anything is unclear, email us. The address is at the bottom.

What Slop Bin does

Slop Bin is a browser extension that lets you use a humorous “bin mode” on supported websites (v1: LinkedIn) to visually dismiss feed posts you choose. It is not a serious productivity or surveillance product, and it is not affiliated with LinkedIn.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores information locally in your browser using Chrome’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local), including:

  • Whether Bin mode is turned on
  • How many free removals you have used
  • Whether a license is activated and an optional opaque token returned when you verify a purchase

We do not require an account to use the extension.

License verification

If you enter a license key, the extension may send that key to Gumroad (our license provider) over HTTPS to confirm your purchase. We do not process payments inside the extension; checkout happens on Gumroad’s site, governed by Gumroad’s own privacy practices.

What we do not do (v1)

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not collect the full text of your feed posts on our servers for analytics.
  • We do not run a backend that builds a profile of what you read, beyond what Gumroad needs to validate a license key when you activate.
